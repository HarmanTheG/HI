Core Mechanics

Combat System

  • M1 String: 4-hit basic attack combos that scale damage and setup skills.
  • Block & Guard Break: Standard blocking negates normal M1s, but specific heavy moves break guards.
  • Evasive/Down Tech: Timed escapes allow players to break out of devastating combos.

Awakening (Mode)

  • Filling the Awakening Meter unlocks a character's "Awakening" state (e.g., Super Saiyan, Hollowification).
  • Grants 4 brand-new, extremely powerful moves.
  • Can completely turn the tide of a losing match if timed correctly.

Roster Diversity

  • Features characters across dozens of anime (Dragon Ball, Naruto, Bleach, One Piece, Jojo, Fate, etc.).
  • Split into DLCs, Roster characters, and Unlockables via Gold.

The Prestige Grind

What is Prestige?

Once a player reaches Account Level 100, they gain the ability to Prestige. Resetting your level back to 1 gives high-status rewards and proves ultimate dedication to the game.

  • Requirements: Reach Level 100 via grinding Ranked or Public matches.
  • Rewards: Exclusive Prestige skins, unique gold titles, prestige icons, and flex status in lobbies.
  • Multi-Prestige: Players can prestige multiple times to unlock rarer badges and show off their thousands of hours played.

Skins & Cosmetics

Skin Rarity Tiers

Skins alter character models, sound effects, and visual animations.

  • Common / Rare: Color palette swaps or minor design changes.
  • Legendary: Complete model overhauls (e.g., genderbends, alternate universe versions).
  • Prestige / Exclusive: Unlocked only via hard grinding or special events.

Gold & Skin Crates

The primary gacha loop driving player economy.

  • Earn Gold by winning matches and landing hits.
  • Spend Gold on opening Skin Crates to roll for high-tier skins.
  • Unlocking legendary skins provides immense cosmetic flex in public arenas.

Why Is ABA So Addicting?

1. High Skill Ceiling & Combo Freedom

Unlike casual button-mashers, ABA relies heavily on precision timing, jump-cancels, combo extensions, and baiting out enemy evasives. Mastering a complex character feels incredibly rewarding.

2. The Ranked 1v1 Dopamine Loop

Ranked play offers competitive matchmaking with MMR points. The constant thrill of climbing ranks, reading opponents, and landing clutch Awakenings keeps competitive players hooked.

3. Fan-Service & Anime Accuracy

Move-sets are crafted with extreme care and respect for source anime material. Executing iconic anime moments frame-for-frame against real players creates unparalleled satisfaction.

4. Endless Progression

Between unlocking every character, grinding skin crates, climbing ranked leaderboards, and striving for Prestige status, there is always another goal to chase.

What are Gold Skins?

Players typically earn a gold skin by spending enough play time or grinding matches with a specific character (often cited by the community as requiring around 3 hours of active gameplay per character.) Because they require a significant time investment and dedication to master or grind a single character, gold skins act as a status symbol or badge of honor in-game.Almost every character in the game's massive anime roster—spanning series like One Piece, Naruto, Dragon Ball Z, and JoJo's Bizarre Adventure—has a unique gold skin variant.

What is a Prestige?

How Prestiging Works

Requirement: You must be at least level 100.
Process: Talk to the NPC named Mr. Random at the back of the lobby.
Cost: Prestiging subtracts 99 levels from your current level, returning you back toward level 1.
The Reward: You are shown two random prestige characters and must pick one to permanently unlock and play. Subsequent prestiges also give experience multipliers to make leveling faster next time.
